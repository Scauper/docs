Kaspert's **Verkoop** module gives your youth organisation a fully featured online webshop — no separate tool, no third-party plugin, and no technical know-how required. From a waffle sale to a summer-camp registration, you can create a public order page in minutes, start accepting online payments, and watch every completed order land automatically in your kasboek as an income transaction. Everything lives under one roof alongside your ledger, member records, and expense approvals.

## What you can sell

Kaspert's webshop is designed around the real-world fundraising and activity needs of Flemish youth groups. Common use cases include:

- **Event tickets** — fuif (youth disco) entry, soep-en-broodjes nights, spaghetti evenings, quiz nights
- **Camp registrations** — summer camp (kamp) sign-ups with optional size or dietary questions
- **Merchandise** — branded hoodies, scarves, badges, or anything your group produces
- **Activity slots** — day trips, workshops, or excursions with limited capacity per slot
- **Fundraising products** — candles, chocolates, raffle tickets, or whatever keeps the treasury healthy

## Kasboek integration

When a buyer pays online via Stripe or Bancontact Pro, Kaspert automatically records an income transaction in your kasboek — no manual bookkeeping required. The transaction is labelled with the sale name and linked to the correct werkjaar, so your treasurer can reconcile income at a glance. For orders paid by bank transfer or on-site, you mark them as paid manually in the Bestellingen tab; your treasurer can then add the corresponding kasboek entry.

## Transaction fees

Kaspert keeps the platform's own transaction fee as low as possible so more money stays with your organisation. The fee applies on top of any Stripe or Bancontact Pro processing costs.

| Plan | Kaspert transaction fee |
|------|------------------------|
| Free | 1% per order (max €0.50) |
| Basic | 0.5% per order (max €0.25) |
| Pro | 0% |
| Premium | 0% |

<Info>
  The Kaspert transaction fee is separate from Stripe's own payment processing fee (typically ~1.4% + €0.25 for European cards). Bancontact Pro pricing depends on your organisation's own Bancontact Pro business agreement.
</Info>
