Kaspert's **Verkoop** module gives your youth organisation a fully featured online webshop — no separate tool, no third-party plugin, and no technical know-how required. From a waffle sale to a summer-camp registration, you can create a public order page in minutes, start accepting online payments, and watch every completed order land automatically in your kasboek as an income transaction. Everything lives under one roof alongside your ledger, member records, and expense approvals.

## What you can sell

Kaspert's webshop is designed around the real-world fundraising and activity needs of Flemish youth groups. Common use cases include:

- **Event tickets** — fuif (youth disco) entry, soep-en-broodjes nights, spaghetti evenings, quiz nights
- **Camp registrations** — summer camp (kamp) sign-ups with optional size or dietary questions
- **Merchandise** — branded hoodies, scarves, badges, or anything your group produces
- **Activity slots** — day trips, workshops, or excursions with limited capacity per slot
- **Fundraising products** — candles, chocolates, raffle tickets, or whatever keeps the treasury healthy

## Key features

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Public order page" icon="globe">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Custom product questions" icon="circle-question">

  <Card title="Discount codes" icon="tag">
    Create percentage or fixed-amount promo codes with optional usage limits and expiry dates. Buyers enter their code at checkout before paying.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Order confirmation emails" icon="envelope">
    Kaspert automatically sends a branded confirmation email to the buyer after each order. You can customise the subject, intro, and closing text.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AI copy assistant" icon="sparkles">
    Not sure what to write? Use the built-in AI assistant to generate a page title and description for your sale in seconds.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your organisation's branding" icon="palette">
    Your logo and primary colour are automatically applied to the public order page, making it look professional and familiar to parents and members.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Automatic kasboek integration

Every completed order is recorded as an income transaction in your kasboek automatically — no manual bookkeeping required. The transaction is linked to the correct werkjaar and labelled with the sale name, so your treasurer can always reconcile income at a glance.

## Transaction fees

Kaspert keeps the platform's own transaction fee as low as possible so more money stays with your organisation. The fee applies on top of any Stripe or Payconiq processing costs.

| Plan | Kaspert transaction fee |
|------|------------------------|
| Free | 1% per order (max €0.50) |
| Basic | 0.5% per order (max €0.25) |
| Pro | 0% |
| Premium | 0% |

<Info>
  The Kaspert transaction fee is separate from Stripe's own payment processing fee (typically ~1.4% + €0.25 for European cards on Stripe Connect). Payconiq pricing depends on your own Payconiq business agreement.
</Info>
