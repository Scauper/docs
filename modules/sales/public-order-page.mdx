The public order page is what your buyers see — parents ordering camp tickets, neighbours buying waffles for the fundraiser, or members picking up their new hoodie. It is a clean, mobile-first page hosted by Kaspert that reflects your organisation's branding and requires no login whatsoever from the buyer. You share a single link, and the rest takes care of itself.

## The public order URL

Every sale you publish gets its own unique URL:

```
https://kaspert.be/bestellen/{companySlug}/{saleSlug}
```

For example, a Chiro group called *De Beemdratten* selling waffles might have:

```
https://kaspert.be/bestellen/beemdratten/wafelverkoop-2025
```

You can find and copy this URL at the top of your sale's detail page in the Kaspert dashboard. Share it anywhere — a newsletter, a Facebook event, a printed QR code, or a WhatsApp message.

<Info>
  **No account required for buyers.** Anyone with the link can browse and order. Kaspert does not ask buyers to create a profile or remember a password.
</Info>

## What buyers see on the page

When a buyer opens the order URL, they land on a page that includes:

- Your **organisation logo** and primary brand colour
- The **hero title and description** you set under Instellingen → Lay-out
- An optional **hero image** and gallery photos
- All your **products** grouped by category (if you created categories), with names, descriptions, prices, and product photos
- A **shopping cart** that updates in real time as the buyer selects items

## The buying flow

<Steps>

### Open the URL and browse

The buyer opens the link on any device. Products are displayed in a grid or list depending on screen size. If a product has a stock limit set and is sold out, it is shown as unavailable.

### Select products and quantities

The buyer uses **+** and **−** buttons to set the quantity for each product. The running cart total updates instantly. If you set a per-order limit on a product, the quantity picker stops at that maximum.

### Answer custom questions

If any product has questions configured (e.g. "Grootte T-shirt", "Vleeskeuze"), a form appears when the buyer adds that product to their cart. Required questions must be answered before the buyer can proceed to checkout. Optional questions can be skipped.

Question types the buyer may encounter:

| Type | What the buyer does |
|------|---------------------|
| Single choice | Selects one option from a radio list |
| Multiple choice | Ticks one or more checkboxes |
| Quantity per option | Enters a number next to each option (e.g. 2× Kip, 1× Worst) |
| Number | Types a number |
| Free text | Types a free-form answer |
| Checkbox | Ticks a single yes/no box |

Some answer options may add a price surcharge — this is shown next to the option label.

### Enter a discount code (optional)

In the cart summary, the buyer can expand the **Kortingscode** field and type a promo code. Kaspert validates the code immediately:

- If valid, the discount amount appears as a deduction in the cart total.
- If invalid (wrong code, expired, or limit reached), an error message explains why.

### Enter contact details

The buyer fills in their **name** and **email address** — these are used to send the order confirmation. Depending on the settings you chose under Instellingen → Klantvelden, they may also be asked for a **phone number** or a **department/section** (tak).

If you have enabled delivery, the buyer also enters a delivery address at this step.

### Choose a payment method

The buyer sees only the payment methods you have enabled for this sale:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Bank transfer" icon="building-columns">
    The buyer receives your IBAN and a structured payment reference in the confirmation email. No online payment is taken at this step — the order is placed immediately and the buyer pays later.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Payconiq QR" icon="qrcode">
    A Payconiq QR code is displayed on screen. The buyer opens the Payconiq app, scans the code, and confirms the payment. Kaspert checks the payment status automatically and updates the order.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Stripe (card / Bancontact)" icon="credit-card">
    The buyer is redirected to a Stripe-hosted checkout page to enter their card details or select Bancontact. After payment, they are returned to the Kaspert confirmation page.
  </Card>
  <Card title="On-site payment" icon="hand-holding-euro">
    No payment is taken online. The order is recorded with a "te betalen ter plaatse" status. The buyer pays at pick-up.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Complete payment and receive confirmation

After a successful payment (or after placing an on-site order), the buyer is redirected to a **confirmation page** that shows their order number and a summary of what they ordered.

At the same moment, Kaspert sends an **order confirmation email** to the address the buyer provided. The email contains:

- The order number and sale name
- A full product and price breakdown
- Payment instructions (IBAN + reference, Payconiq QR, or on-site notice — depending on the payment method)
- Your custom intro and closing text

</Steps>

## What happens on your side

Every completed order appears in your Kaspert dashboard immediately:

- **Verkoop → [sale name] → Bestellingen** — the order row shows buyer name, products, total, and payment status in real time.
- **Kasboek** — a corresponding income transaction is automatically created and linked to the correct werkjaar, so your treasurer's ledger stays up to date without any manual entry.
- **Notification email** — if you configured notification addresses under Instellingen → Meldingen, you receive an email alert for each new order.

For Stripe payments, funds are batched and paid out to your linked bank account on Stripe's standard payout schedule (typically T+2 business days for Belgian accounts).

<Note>
  **Payconiq payment status.** When a buyer pays by Payconiq, Kaspert polls the payment status in the background. The status in your dashboard updates to `betaald` as soon as Payconiq confirms the transaction. This usually takes only a few seconds, but may occasionally take up to a minute.
</Note>

<Warning>
  **Inactive sales show a closed page.** If you toggle the **Actief** switch off (or if the sale has passed its automatic close time), the public order page displays a "closed" message and new orders cannot be placed. Existing orders and completed payments are not affected in any way — only new orders are blocked.
</Warning>

## Fulfilment and pick-up slots

If your sale involves physical pick-up (e.g. a waffle sale with multiple collection time windows), you can configure **fulfilment slots** under Instellingen → Afhalen & leveren. Each slot has a label (e.g. `17u – 18u`) and an optional capacity. Buyers choose a slot at checkout and the slot's booking count is updated in real time, preventing overbooking.

For sales with home delivery, you can enable the delivery address field and configure a delivery cost that is added to the order total automatically.
