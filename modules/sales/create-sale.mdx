Creating a sale in Kaspert takes only a few minutes. You define the products you want to sell, set your prices, optionally collect extra information from buyers (such as a T-shirt size or a dietary preference), choose how buyers will pay, and then flip a switch to go live. The resulting public order page is a clean, mobile-friendly URL you can drop into a newsletter, a Facebook post, or a WhatsApp group — no buyer account required.

## Navigate to Verkoop

Open your Kaspert dashboard and click **Verkoop** in the sidebar (or go directly to `/verkoop`). This is the home of all your sales, both active and archived.

## Setting up your sale

<Steps>

### Create a new sale

Click the **Nieuwe verkoop** button in the top-right corner. Enter a descriptive name for your sale — for example, `Wafelverkoop maart 2025` or `Kampinschrijving zomer 2025` — and click **Aanmaken**. Kaspert generates a unique URL slug from the name automatically; you can change it later in the sale's settings.

### Add products

Inside your new sale, click **Product toevoegen** to add your first item. Each product has the following fields:

| Field | Required | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **Name** | ✅ | The product name shown on the order page, e.g. `Wafel`, `Groot pakket`, `Kampticket` |
| **Price** | ✅ | Price per unit in euro |
| **Description** | ✗ | A short description shown beneath the product name |
| **Stock limit** | ✗ | Maximum total units available across all orders — leave empty for unlimited |
| **Per-order limit** | ✗ | Maximum quantity a single buyer can add to their cart |

Repeat this step for every product you want to include. You can drag and drop products to reorder them on the public page.

<Tip>
  Grouping related products into **categories** (e.g. "Dranken" and "Eten") keeps the order page tidy when you have more than four or five items. Add a category from the sale detail view before adding products.
</Tip>

### Add custom questions (optional)

If you need extra information from buyers for a specific product — for example, a T-shirt size, a meat choice for a barbecue, or a child's name for a camp — open the product and click **Vraag toevoegen** under the **Vragen** tab.

Each question has:

- **Label** — the question text shown to the buyer, e.g. `Grootte T-shirt`
- **Help text** — optional extra explanation shown beneath the label
- **Question type** — choose from:
  - **Single choice** — buyer picks exactly one option from a list
  - **Multiple choice** — buyer picks one or more options (min/max selectable)
  - **Quantity per option** — buyer enters a quantity for each option (e.g. 2× chicken, 1× beef)
  - **Number** — buyer types a number (with optional min/max validation)
  - **Free text** — buyer types a free-form answer
  - **Checkbox** — a simple yes/no tick
- **Required** — toggle on to block checkout until the buyer answers

For choice-type questions, add the individual answer options (e.g. `XS`, `S`, `M`, `L`, `XL`). You can also attach a price surcharge to any option — for example, an `XXL` option at +€2.

### Configure payment methods

Go to **Instellingen → Betaalmethodes** inside your sale. Kaspert supports four payment methods — enable whichever ones apply to your sale:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Bank transfer (overschrijving)">
    The buyer receives your IBAN and a payment reference in the confirmation email. Enter your **IBAN** and **beneficiary name** (your organisation's name) in the settings. This method has no processing fee beyond Kaspert's own transaction fee.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="EPC QR code">
    A QR code following the European Payment Council (EPC) standard is shown at checkout. The buyer scans it with any Belgian banking app (KBC, ING, Belfius, etc.) to pre-fill the transfer details. No separate app subscription is required for this method.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Stripe (card & Bancontact via Stripe)">
    Buyers pay online with a credit card or Bancontact via Stripe. Funds are paid out directly to your organisation's linked bank account. You must complete Stripe onboarding first — see the note below.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="On-site payment">
    No online payment is taken. The buyer places an order and pays in person at pick-up or delivery. The order is recorded with a pending (Openstaand) status; you mark it as paid manually in the Bestellingen tab once you collect the money.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  **Stripe Connect setup required before accepting card payments.** Go to **Beheer → Organisatie → Betalingen** and click **Stripe koppelen** to start the onboarding flow. You will need your organisation's legal name, address, and bank account details. Once Stripe has verified and activated your account, the Stripe payment method becomes available to enable on your sales.
</Note>

### Write your shop description

Go to **Instellingen → Lay-out**. Here you can set:

- **Page title** — the large heading displayed at the top of your order page
- **Description** — a paragraph below the title that explains the sale, event details, pick-up info, or anything else buyers should know
- **Hero image** — an optional full-width banner photo (recommended: landscape, at least 1200 px wide, max 5 MB)
- **Gallery photos** — optional additional photos shown at the bottom of the page

Not sure what to write? Click the **Schrijf mijn teksten** button to open the AI copy assistant. It generates a title and description based on your sale name and a brief prompt — you can edit the result before saving.

A live preview of your public order page is embedded directly in the layout editor so you can see changes before saving.

### Set the sale as active

Back in your sale overview, find the **Actief** toggle and turn it on. This makes the public order page accessible. While a sale is inactive, the order page shows a "closed" message and buyers cannot place orders.

You can also configure automatic open and close times under **Instellingen → Open/sluit-tijden**. For example, set the shop to open on the day you publish your newsletter and close at midnight on the last order day.

### Share the public link

Copy the public URL shown at the top of your sale detail page. The URL format is:

```
https://kaspert.be/bestellen/{companySlug}/{saleSlug}
```

Share this link in your communication channels — a newsletter, Facebook event, WhatsApp group, or printed QR code. Buyers can open it on any device without logging in.

</Steps>

## Managing orders

Once buyers start placing orders, click on your sale in the Verkoop overview to see the **Bestellingen** tab. For each order you can see:

- Order number and creation date
- Buyer name and email address
- Products ordered and quantities
- Total amount and payment status (**Betaald** or **Openstaand**)
- Payment method used
- Answers to any custom questions

Click an order row to expand the full detail, including line items, question answers, and any discount code applied.

<Tip>
  Click **Exporteren** to download all orders as an Excel file. This is useful for preparing pick-up lists, seating plans, or reporting to your group's national association.
</Tip>

## Confirmation email

Kaspert automatically sends an order confirmation email to the buyer immediately after checkout. The email includes:

- The buyer's name and order number
- A full product overview with quantities and prices
- Payment instructions (IBAN and payment reference for bank transfer, or EPC QR code if the EPC QR method is enabled) — or an on-site payment notice
- Your custom intro and closing text

To customise the email, go to **Instellingen → Bevestigingsmail**. You can set a custom subject line and edit the intro and closing paragraphs using the rich text editor. The product table, totals, and payment block are generated automatically and cannot be removed.

You can also set one or more **notification email addresses** under **Instellingen → Meldingen** to receive an alert each time a new order arrives.
