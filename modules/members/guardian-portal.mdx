The guardian portal is a personalised web page that shows a family's existing children and lets guardians re-enroll them for the new season with a single click. From a guardian's perspective, the experience is seamless: they receive an email, click a personal link, and find a pre-filled form with all their children's names, birth dates, and addresses already filled in from last year. They review the details, select the correct tak, confirm, and they're done — no account required, no re-typing data from scratch.

## How the guardian portal works

The portal is accessible at:

```text
https://kaspert.be/gezin/{companySlug}?token={personalToken}
```

Each guardian receives a **unique, time-limited link** containing a cryptographically secure token. The token is valid for **30 days** from the moment it is generated. When a guardian opens their link they see:

- Their own contact details (name, email, phone)
- A card for each child linked to their guardian record from the previous season
- The enrollment status and payment status for each child in the current werkjaar
- Buttons to update personal details and the child's medical record

If a child is **already enrolled** for the current werkjaar, their card shows a green "Lidgeld betaald" or an open payment balance with payment options. To enroll a child who is not yet enrolled for the current year, the guardian clicks **Kind (her)inschrijven**, which opens the standard enrollment form with their family data pre-filled.

### What guardians can update

From the guardian portal, parents can update the following without contacting you:

- Child's address (street, postal code, municipality)
- Photo consent
- Guardian's phone number
- Full medical record (with explicit consent checkbox)

These updates take effect immediately in the Kaspert member database.

### Online payment from the portal

If your organisation has Stripe or Bancontact (Payconiq) connected, the guardian portal also shows an **online pay** button per child next to the membership fee. Guardians can pay directly from their family page without logging in.

## Sending re-enrollment invitations

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make sure enrollment is open">
    Before sending invitations, confirm that the enrollment toggle is switched on in **Leden → Instellingen → Inschrijvingen open**. Guardians who receive a link cannot do anything useful if enrollment is closed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Leden → Instellingen">
    Navigate to **Leden** (`/leden`) and click the **Instellingen** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Nodig alle ouders uit voor herinschrijving'">
    Scroll to the **Herinschrijving** section and click the button. A confirmation dialog explains how many guardians will be contacted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Verstuur uitnodigingen** in the dialog. Kaspert immediately begins sending personalised emails to every guardian on file who has a valid email address and has not been invited in the last 7 days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the result">
    After sending, a toast notification shows how many emails were **sent** and how many were **skipped**. Skipped guardians either had no valid email address or were already invited recently.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the invitation email contains

Each guardian receives a personalised email with:

- A greeting using their first name
- A short explanation that the new season is starting and their family data is ready
- A prominent **Open mijn gezinslink** button
- The full link in plain text (for guardians whose email client blocks buttons)
- A note that the link is valid for 30 days

The email is sent from `inschrijving@kaspert.be` on behalf of your organisation, with your organisation name as the displayed sender.

## Rate limiting

<Note>
  To prevent accidental spam, Kaspert enforces a limit of **one invitation per guardian per 7 days**. If a guardian was already sent a link in the past 7 days — whether by a previous bulk send or by the system during enrollment — they are automatically skipped. The result toast shows the exact count of skipped guardians.
</Note>

## Requesting a new link (guardian self-service)

If a guardian's link has expired or they have lost it, they can request a fresh one themselves. On the public enrollment form (`kaspert.be/inschrijven/{slug}`), a **"Was je kind vorig jaar al lid?"** card appears on the first step. The guardian enters their email address and clicks **Stuur mijn gezinslink**. If the email is found in your guardian records, a new personal link is sent immediately.

The same self-service request is also available on the guardian portal page itself when the token is invalid or expired — the page shows a form to request a new link.

<Tip>
  Send re-enrollment invitations two to four weeks before the start of the new season. Make sure your enrollment form is fully configured — correct takken, up-to-date membership fees, and the right IBAN — before you send, so that guardians see accurate information when they arrive at their family portal.
</Tip>
