The Kasboek is the financial heart of Kaspert. Every euro your group receives or spends flows through this module — whether you type it in by hand, import it from your bank, or reconcile it against a member payment. The live balance summary at the top of the page updates instantly as you add or edit transactions, so your treasurer always has an accurate picture of where the group stands financially, all scoped to the active **werkjaar** (fiscal season).

## What you can do in the Kasboek

- **Book income and expenses manually** — record any financial movement in seconds, with an optional receipt photo or PDF attached as evidence.
- **Import bank transactions from a CSV file** — download your statement from KBC, Belfius, or ING and upload it directly into Kaspert. Duplicate detection prevents double-counting. *(Pro and Premium plans only.)*
- **Organise transactions with categories** — tag every booking with a category (Kamp, Lidgeld, Fuif, etc.) to keep your records clean and reportable.
- **View a live balance summary per werkjaar** — the three-card summary at the top of the page shows total income, total expenses, and the net balance for the selected season at a glance.
- **Generate an annual report PDF** — produce a ready-to-share financial overview for your *algemene vergadering* (annual meeting) with one click.
- **Analyse spending by category** — the Analyse tab breaks down income and expenses per category so you can spot where money is going across the year.

## Werkjaar filtering

<Note>
  Every transaction in the Kasboek belongs to a **werkjaar** determined by its booking date. When you switch the werkjaar selector in the top-right corner of the page, Kaspert filters all transactions to those whose date falls within that season's start and end date. Transactions entered with a date outside the selected range will not appear in the list or the balance summary until you switch to the correct werkjaar.
</Note>
