Categories are the labels you attach to every transaction in your kasboek. Without them, your ledger is just a list of numbers — with them, you can instantly see how much your group spent on camp, how much came in through membership fees, or what portion of your budget went to transport. Good category hygiene pays off when you generate the annual report or present your finances at the *algemene vergadering*: clear, consistent categories make the category breakdown instantly readable for every board member.

## Managing categories

Navigate to **Kasboek → Categorieën** tab to manage your organisation's categories. Changes take effect immediately and are shared across all users in your organisation.

### Create a new category

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Categorieën tab">
    On the Kasboek page (`/dashboard`), click the **Categorieën** tab in the navigation bar below the balance summary.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the category name">
    Enter the name in the input field at the top (e.g. `Kamp`, `Lidgeld`, or `Fuif`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Toevoegen** or press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. The category appears in the active list and is immediately available in the transaction form dropdown.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use short, descriptive names — they appear in dropdown menus, the transaction list, the category breakdown chart, and in exported reports. Long names get truncated in narrow columns.
</Tip>

### Rename a category

Click the **pencil icon** next to any active category to enter edit mode. Type the new name and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or click the checkmark to confirm. Kaspert automatically updates **all existing transactions** that reference the old category name, so your historical records stay consistent.

### Archive a category

Click the **trash icon** next to a category to remove it. Kaspert checks whether any transactions currently use this category:

- **No transactions linked** — the category is permanently deleted.
- **Transactions exist** — the category is **archived** (soft-deleted) instead of deleted. Archived categories no longer appear in the dropdown when you book a new transaction, but all existing transactions retain their category label. Their amounts still appear in the category breakdown report.

To restore an archived category, scroll to the **Gearchiveerd** section at the bottom of the Categorieën tab and click the **restore icon** next to the category you want to reactivate.

<Note>
  Categories are scoped to your **organisation**, not to an individual werkjaar. A category you create this season will be available in all future seasons and will appear in the breakdowns for all past seasons where it was used.
</Note>

## Suggested categories for a youth organisation

If you are setting up Kaspert for a new group, here are some common category names used by Flemish youth organisations:

| Category | Typical use |
|---|---|
| `Kamp` | Summer camp costs: food, site rental, activities |
| `Lidgeld` | Annual membership fee income |
| `Uitstap` | Day trips and excursions |
| `Materiaal` | Equipment, craft supplies, tools |
| `Sponsor` | Donations and sponsorship income |
| `Fuif` | Youth party income and expenses |
| `Voedsel` | Weekly meeting snacks and food |
| `Transport` | Bus hire, fuel, travel costs |
| `Verzekering` | Insurance premiums |

You are not required to use these names — create whatever categories match how your group organises its finances.

## Category breakdown report

Switch to the **Analyse** tab in the Kasboek to see a visual breakdown of income and expenses per category for the selected werkjaar. Each category is shown with its total amount alongside a proportional bar, making it easy to compare spending across areas at a glance. The breakdown is split into separate sections for income categories and expense categories.

<Info>
  The category breakdown only includes transactions in the currently selected werkjaar. Switch the werkjaar selector in the top-right corner to compare different seasons.
</Info>
