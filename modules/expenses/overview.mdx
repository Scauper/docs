Managing expenses in a youth organisation has always been a paper-heavy headache. Leaders buy food for a camp, materials for a workshop, or petrol for a transport run — and then hand a crumpled receipt to the treasurer at the next meeting (or, more often, forget to entirely). Kaspert's expense module replaces that chaotic paper trail with a structured, fully digital approval flow that saves your treasurer hours of back-and-forth every werkjaar.

## The problem it solves

In a typical youth group, dozens of leaders and helpers make small purchases throughout the season. Each one needs to be documented, reviewed, and reimbursed. Without a system, receipts get lost, amounts get disputed, and the treasurer ends up chasing people for details months after the fact. Kaspert gives every member a simple way to submit a receipt from their phone — no app download, no account required — and gives the treasurer a single dashboard to review, approve, and pay everything in one place.

## How the expense flow works

<Steps>
  <Step title="A member submits an expense">
    Anyone who made a purchase on behalf of the group opens the public submission link your admin shares — `https://kaspert.be/{companySlug}/onkosten` — or uses the Onkosten page inside the Kaspert app if they have an account. No login is needed for the public form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They fill in the details and attach a receipt">
    The submitter enters their name, Belgian IBAN (for reimbursement), a description of the purchase, the amount in euros, and the date. They attach a photo of the receipt or upload a PDF — straight from their phone camera if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI reads the receipt automatically">
    If your organisation is on the Basic plan or higher, Kaspert's AI scans the receipt image and pre-fills the amount, date, description, and category for the submitter. They should always verify these values before hitting submit, but in most cases the scan is accurate enough to save significant typing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The expense appears in the Onkosten dashboard">
    The submitted expense shows up immediately under **Onkosten** (`/onkosten`) in the admin panel, tagged as pending. The treasurer sees the submitter's name, IBAN, amount, description, date, and a link to view the receipt photo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The admin reviews and approves or rejects">
    The treasurer clicks the expense, checks the receipt, adjusts any details if necessary (such as assigning a kasboek category), and either approves or rejects it. If your kasboek module is enabled, they must choose a category before approving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approved expenses are booked automatically">
    Approving an expense removes the pending flag and adds the transaction directly to the kasboek as an expense entry, using the category and date from the submission. The receipt photo URL is stored alongside the transaction as proof.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Plan requirements

<Note>
  The Onkosten module is available on the **Basic plan and higher** (€4.99/month). OCR-powered receipt scanning — which automatically pre-fills the amount, date, description, and category from a photo — requires the **Basic plan or higher** and must be enabled in your organisation's settings. The Free plan does not include expense submissions.
</Note>
