Kaspert is free to start and scales with your group's needs. Every new organisation begins on the **Free** plan — no credit card required, no time limit. When your group needs more modules, more admins, or a lower transaction fee on your webshop sales, you can upgrade to Basic, Pro, or Premium at any time. This page explains what each plan includes, how billing works, and how to manage your subscription.

## Webshop transaction fees

When members or parents place orders through your Kaspert webshop (Verkoop module), a small platform fee applies on Free and Basic plans. Pro and Premium users pay no platform fee.

| Plan | Transaction fee | Maximum fee per order |
|------|-----------------|-----------------------|
| **Free** | 1% of order value | €0.50 |
| **Basic** | 0.5% of order value | €0.25 |
| **Pro** | 0% | — |
| **Premium** | 0% | — |

<Info>
  Transaction fees are deducted from your Kaspert account balance, not charged separately. You can view your current balance and commission history in **Beheer > Abonnement > Tegoed & commissie**.
</Info>

## How to upgrade your plan

1. In the top navigation, click **Beheer** to open the admin panel (`/admin`).
2. Go to the **Abonnement** tab.
3. In the plan comparison table, click the **Kies [plan name]** button for the plan you want.
4. Select **Maandelijks** or **Jaarlijks** billing and confirm your choice.

Upgrades take effect **immediately**. Any newly unlocked modules become available in your navigation bar right away.

## How to downgrade or cancel

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Downgrade to a lower plan">
    1. Go to **Beheer > Abonnement**.
    2. Click the plan you want to switch to.
    3. Confirm the downgrade.

    Downgrades take effect at the **end of your current billing period** — you retain access to your current plan's features until then.

    <Warning>
      If your new plan does not include one or more modules you currently use, those modules will be **disabled automatically** when the downgrade takes effect. Your data is not deleted — it is preserved and will reappear if you re-upgrade. However, new entries cannot be added to disabled modules. Disable modules manually before downgrading if you want to control the timing.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Cancel your subscription">
    **Monthly plan:** Cancel before your next renewal date. Your plan remains active until the end of the current billing month.

    **Yearly plan:** Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current yearly period. You will not be charged for the next year.

    To cancel:
    1. Go to **Beheer > Abonnement**.
    2. Click **Abonnement opzeggen** and confirm.

    <Warning>
      After cancellation, your account reverts to the **Free** plan. Any modules not included in the Free plan are disabled. Make sure to **export your data** (transactions, member records, expense reports) before cancelling a paid plan if you want to keep a local copy.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Frequently asked questions

<Accordion title="Do I need a credit card to start on the Free plan?">
  No. The Free plan is completely free with no credit card required. You can create an organisation, set up your kasboek, run a webshop, and manage your team without providing any payment information.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I switch plans at any time?">
  Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time from **Beheer > Abonnement**.

  - **Upgrades** take effect immediately — you gain access to the new modules and features right away.
  - **Downgrades** take effect at the end of your current billing period. You keep your current plan's features until then.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens to my data if I cancel?">
  Your data is retained for **30 days** after cancellation. During this window you can re-activate your subscription and pick up exactly where you left off. After 30 days, data may be permanently deleted in accordance with our data retention policy.

  **We strongly recommend exporting your data before cancelling** — especially your kasboek transactions (CSV export available from the kasboek overview) and any member records. You can initiate exports from the relevant module screens while your paid plan is still active.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is VAT included in the listed prices?">
  All prices listed on this page are exclusive of VAT. Applicable Belgian VAT is added at checkout based on your organisation's VAT status. Non-profit youth organisations (vzw) may be eligible for VAT exemptions — consult your accountant or fiscal advisor if you are unsure.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I get an invoice for my subscription?">
  Yes. Invoices are generated automatically for each billing cycle and are accessible from **Beheer > Abonnement > Facturen**. You can download them as PDF for your accounting records.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What payment methods are accepted for the subscription?">
  Kaspert accepts major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard) and SEPA direct debit for subscriptions. Payment processing is handled securely by Stripe. Your payment details are never stored on Kaspert's servers.
</Accordion>
