Kaspert uses **Stripe Connect** to let each organisation accept online payments and receive payouts directly into their own bank account. When you connect Stripe to Kaspert, your organisation becomes a Stripe Express account linked to the Kaspert platform — you own the customer relationship and funds flow directly to you, with Kaspert acting as the payment facilitator. This means no manual invoice chasing: buyers pay online at checkout, and Stripe handles the rest.

## What Stripe enables in Kaspert

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Credit and debit cards" icon="credit-card">
    Accept Visa and Mastercard payments from buyers anywhere in the world. Works on desktop and mobile.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bancontact" icon="mobile-screen-button">
    Accept Bancontact — Belgium's most popular payment method — directly from the webshop checkout. No separate Bancontact Pro account needed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Automatic payouts" icon="bank">
    Stripe pays out your collected funds to your organisation's IBAN on a rolling schedule (typically 2-7 business days). Manage payout timing from your Stripe Express dashboard.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Receipts and invoices" icon="receipt">
    Stripe automatically generates a payment receipt for every completed order, which buyers receive by email alongside Kaspert's order confirmation.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to connect Stripe

Only **admins** can complete the Stripe onboarding. Make sure you have your organisation's Belgian IBAN and identity documents handy before you start.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Beheer > Organisatie > Betalingen">
    Open your Kaspert dashboard, navigate to **Beheer (`/admin`)**, open the **Organisatie** tab, and scroll to the **Betalingen** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Verbind met Stripe'">
    Kaspert creates a Stripe Express account for your organisation (or reconnects to an existing one) and generates a secure onboarding link. You are redirected to Stripe's hosted onboarding form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Stripe's onboarding form">
    Fill in your organisation's details, including:
    - Organisation name and type
    - Responsible person's name and date of birth (for identity verification)
    - Belgian IBAN for payouts
    - Mobile number for two-factor authentication

    Stripe may request a copy of a government-issued ID to verify the responsible person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to Kaspert">
    After completing the Stripe form, you are automatically redirected back to Kaspert. The Betalingen section now shows a **Verbonden** status badge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stripe is available at checkout">
    Stripe (card and Bancontact) is now available as a payment method on all your webshop sales. Buyers can select it at checkout immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Stripe onboarding requires a valid Belgian bank account and identity verification of the responsible person. The verification process usually completes instantly, but Stripe may take **1-2 business days** if additional document review is needed. Your webshop will accept payments as soon as verification is complete.
</Warning>

## Transaction fees

Kaspert charges a platform fee on top of Stripe's own processing fees. The Kaspert fee depends on your plan:

| Plan | Kaspert platform fee |
|---|---|
| Free | 1% per order (max 0.50) |
| Basic | 0.5% per order (max 0.25) |
| Pro | 0% |
| Premium | 0% |

These fees are **separate** from Stripe's own processing fees, which Stripe deducts directly from your payouts.

<Info>
  Stripe's typical rates for Belgian organisations are approximately **1.5% + 0.25** per successful card transaction and **1.4% + 0.25** per Bancontact transaction. Check [stripe.com/pricing](https://stripe.com/pricing) for current rates, as Stripe pricing may change.
</Info>

## Accessing your Stripe Express dashboard

Your Stripe Express dashboard gives you a real-time view of payments, payouts, disputes, and your bank account balance — directly from within Kaspert.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Stripe dashboard link">
    Go to **Beheer > Organisatie > Betalingen** and click **Stripe-dashboard openen**. Kaspert generates a short-lived login link and opens your Stripe Express dashboard in a new tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View payouts and transactions">
    In the Stripe dashboard you can see each payout batch, which transactions are included, any pending disputes or refund requests, and your current payout bank account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update bank account or business details">
    If your organisation changes its IBAN or legal representative, update those details directly in the Stripe Express dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How Stripe integrates with the kasboek

When Stripe confirms a payment for a webshop order, Kaspert automatically creates an **income transaction** in your kasboek with the category `Verkoop`. The description is formatted as `[STRIPE] {customer name} - {order number}`. This keeps your ledger up to date without any manual entry.

Kaspert deduplicates payment confirmations automatically — if the same payment event is received more than once (which can occasionally happen with payment processors), Kaspert detects the duplicate and does not create a second transaction. Your kasboek always reflects the correct number of entries.

## Stripe Connect and data security

<Note>
  Kaspert never stores your customers' card numbers. All card data is handled exclusively by Stripe's PCI-compliant infrastructure. Only the minimal account identifier needed to generate dashboard login links is stored on Kaspert's side, and it is never used for anything other than providing you access to your own Stripe Express dashboard.
</Note>
