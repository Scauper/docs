Kaspert gets your youth group's finances up and running fast. By the end of this guide you will have a live account, a configured organisation, an active werkjaar (season), at least one bookkeeping category, and your very first transaction in the kasboek. If you want to bring a co-admin along for the ride, you will have sent them an invitation too. Let's get started.

<Steps>

  <Step title="Create your account at kaspert.be">
    Open [kaspert.be](https://kaspert.be) in your browser and click **Gratis proberen** in the top navigation bar. You will land on the registration page.

    The registration wizard walks you through two paths:

    - **Ik ben uitgenodigd** — use this if your group's treasurer or lead admin has already sent you an invitation email. Register with the exact email address the invitation was sent to; Kaspert automatically links you to the correct organisation.
    - **Nieuwe vereniging starten** — use this if you are the first person from your group to sign up. You become the founding admin.

    For a brand-new group, choose **Nieuwe vereniging starten**, then:

    1. Fill in your **first name**, **last name**, **phone number**, and **email address**.
    2. Enter your **organisation name** (e.g. "Chiro Sint-Jan Gent").
    3. Choose a **password** (minimum 6 characters).
    4. Accept the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy, complete the security check, and click **Account aanmaken**.

    Kaspert sends a confirmation email to the address you provided. Open that email and click the confirmation link to activate your account. After confirmation you are redirected to the login screen — sign in with your email and password.

    <Tip>
      If you registered via an invitation link shared by your group (a URL containing `?ref=…`), a **€20 welcome credit** is automatically added to your new organisation's balance.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Name your organisation and set your branding">
    After your first login, Kaspert opens the onboarding wizard automatically. On the **Jullie profiel** step you can refine how your organisation looks across public-facing pages and outgoing emails:

    - **URL slug** — a short, URL-safe identifier for your group (e.g. `chiro-sint-jan`). Kaspert pre-fills a suggestion based on your organisation name; you can adjust it here. It appears in public webshop links: `kaspert.be/bestellen/chiro-sint-jan/naam-verkoop`.
    - **Logo** — upload a PNG, JPG, or SVG (max 2 MB). Click **Upload logo**, select your file, and Kaspert stores it immediately.
    - **Primary colour** — click the colour swatch to open the colour picker, or type a hex code directly (e.g. `#E63946` for red). This colour is used as the accent colour across your dashboard and public pages.

    None of these fields are mandatory right now — you can always update them later under **Beheer > Organisatie**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete the onboarding wizard">
    The wizard runs through five steps before dropping you into your dashboard. Two of these need your attention now:

    **Choose your modules (Step 1)**

    Toggle on the features your group wants to use. Kaspert comes with five modules:

    | Module | What it does | Minimum plan |
    |--------|-------------|--------------|
    | Kasboek | Income & expense ledger, reports, bank import | Free |
    | Verkoop | Online webshop & order pages | Free |
    | Betalingsopvolging | Track who owes what (lidgeld, bar tabs) | Basic |
    | Onkosten | Member expense submissions with receipt OCR | Basic |
    | Leden | Member admin, enrolments, medical records | Basic |

    You can enable or disable any module later in **Beheer > Modules**.

    **Set your first werkjaar (Step 4)**

    A **werkjaar** is the fiscal season your transactions, payments, and reports are grouped under. Youth groups in Flanders typically run from **1 September** to **31 August**.

    - **Label** — use the format `2024-2025` so it is immediately recognisable in filters and reports.
    - **Start date** — e.g. `2024-09-01`.
    - **End date** — e.g. `2025-08-31`.

    Click **Volgende** to save the werkjaar. Once saved, a green confirmation badge appears. You can add more werkjaren later under **Beheer > Organisatie**.

    <Note>
      The onboarding wizard runs only once per organisation. All settings you configure here — modules, branding, werkjaar — can be changed later in the **Beheer** section.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Book your first transaction">
    After completing the wizard you land on the kasboek dashboard at `/dashboard`. You are now ready to record your first financial entry.

    1. Click **Nieuw** (the green button in the top-right corner of the kasboek).
    2. Choose the transaction type:
       - **Inkomst** — money coming in (membership fees, fundraising revenue, grants).
       - **Uitgave** — money going out (camp supplies, equipment, travel).
    3. Fill in the details:
       - **Bedrag** — the amount in euros (e.g. `45.00`).
       - **Omschrijving** — a short description (e.g. "Lidgeld Jana Claes 2024-2025").
       - **Categorie** — select or create a category. Common starting categories: **Lidgeld**, **Kamp**, **Uitstap**, **Materiaal**. If you have not created any yet, click the **+** icon next to the dropdown to add one on the spot.
       - **Datum** — defaults to today; change it if you are recording a past transaction.
    4. Click **Opslaan**. The entry appears instantly in your kasboek, sorted by date.

    <Tip>
      Use descriptive names for categories from the start — they power your annual report and make audits painless. You can archive unused categories later but cannot delete ones that already have transactions linked to them.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite a co-admin">
    Youth group finances are rarely a one-person job. Invite your co-treasurer or group leader so they can help manage the kasboek from day one.

    1. Navigate to **Beheer** (`/admin`) in the top navigation.
    2. Open the **Leden** tab.
    3. Click **Uitnodigen**.
    4. Enter the email address of the person you want to invite and click **Verstuur**.

    Kaspert sends them an invitation email with a personal registration link. When they sign up through that link and choose **Ik ben uitgenodigd**, they are automatically joined to your organisation. You can manage their role (admin or member) from the same Leden tab.

    <Note>
      Invited team members on the **Free** plan are limited to **1 admin**. Upgrade to **Basic** for up to 3 admins, or **Pro/Premium** for unlimited admins.
    </Note>
  </Step>

</Steps>
