Whether you're evaluating Kaspert for the first time or have been using it for a season, this page collects the questions we hear most often. If your question isn't answered here, email **support@kaspert.be** — we typically respond within one business day.

<Accordion title="Is Kaspert free?">
  Yes — Kaspert has a free plan that includes the kasboek (ledger) and the webshop, with no credit card required to sign up.

  If your organisation needs additional features, you can upgrade to a paid plan:

  | Plan | Price | Key extras |
  |---|---|---|
  | Free | 0/month | Kasboek, webshop |
  | Basic | 4.99/month | All modules: expense tracking, payment follow-up, member admin |
  | Pro | 8.99/month | All Basic features + automatic bank CSV import, OCR receipt scanning, 0% webshop fee |
  | Premium | 14.99/month | All Pro features + benchmark reports, unlimited admins, priority support |

  Choosing **yearly billing** gives you approximately **2 months free** compared to paying month to month.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Which organisations can use Kaspert?">
  Kaspert is built specifically for **Chiro, Scouts, KSA, KLJ**, and similar youth organisations in Flanders. The product is designed around the concepts these groups use every day — werkjaar (season), tak (age section), penningmeester (treasurer), and camp budgeting.

  That said, any volunteer group or association that needs to maintain a ledger, track member payments, or run a simple webshop can use Kaspert. If you're unsure whether it fits your needs, start with the free plan and explore.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can multiple people collaborate?">
  Yes. You can invite as many team members as you need via **Beheer > Leden > Uitnodigen**. Each person receives an email invitation and creates their own Kaspert account.

  There are two roles:
  - **Admin** — full access to manage transactions, members, the webshop, expenses, and settings. Typically the penningmeester and group leaders.
  - **Member** — can view the kasboek in read-only mode and submit expense requests for approval. Cannot edit financial data.

  Multiple admins can work in Kaspert simultaneously — there are no conflicts or locking mechanisms.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What's the difference with Excel?">
  Excel is flexible but requires manual effort for almost everything. Kaspert offers several capabilities that are difficult or impossible to replicate in a spreadsheet:

  - **Automatic bank import** — import your KBC or ING CSV export and Kaspert matches transactions to your categories automatically, with duplicate detection.
  - **Digital receipt scanning** — submit expense requests with a photo of the receipt and Kaspert's OCR reads the amount and date.
  - **Payment tracking with fuzzy matching** — Kaspert compares bank transactions against your list of expected payments and suggests matches even when the reference isn't exact.
  - **Integrated webshop** — sell camp spots, group gear, or anything else with an online order form and automatic payment processing.
  - **Simultaneous access** — multiple people can use Kaspert at the same time without overwriting each other's work.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Are there transaction fees on the webshop?">
  Kaspert charges a small platform fee on webshop orders depending on your plan:

  | Plan | Kaspert fee |
  |---|---|
  | Free | 1% per order (max 0.50) |
  | Basic | 0.5% per order (max 0.25) |
  | Pro | 0% |
  | Premium | 0% |

  These fees are separate from the payment processor's own fees (Stripe or Payconiq). Stripe typically charges about 1.5% + 0.25 per transaction for Belgian cards and Bancontact. See the [Stripe reference page](/reference/stripe) for details.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How secure is my data?">
  All data in Kaspert is **encrypted at rest and in transit** (TLS 1.2+). Data is stored in EU-based infrastructure.

  Each organisation's data is isolated — access controls in the database ensure that users can only access data belonging to their own organisation. Only people you explicitly invite can see your organisation's finances, members, or orders.

  Kaspert employees can access data in anonymised or aggregate form for support purposes, but access to individual organisation data requires explicit consent or a support ticket from an admin.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I import bank statements?">
  Yes, with a **Pro or Premium** plan. Navigate to **Kasboek > Bankimport** to upload a CSV export from your bank.

  Kaspert supports exports from **KBC** and **ING** out of the box. Other banks may also work if their CSV contains `Datum` and `Bedrag` columns. Kaspert automatically:

  - Detects the bank format
  - Handles Windows-1252 / ISO-8859-1 character encoding
  - Normalises dates to `YYYY-MM-DD`
  - Detects and flags probable duplicate transactions

  See the [Bank CSV Format reference page](/reference/bank-csv-import) for the full technical specification.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What if I get stuck or need help?">
  You have a few options:

  1. **Check these help pages** — the left navigation has how-to guides for every module.
  2. **Email support** — write to **support@kaspert.be** with a description of your issue and your organisation name. We typically respond within one business day.

  When contacting support, it helps to include:
  - Your organisation name and the email address of your admin account
  - A description of what you were trying to do and what happened instead
  - Any error message you saw (a screenshot is always helpful)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I cancel at any time?">
  Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time from **Beheer > Organisatie > Abonnement**.

  - **Monthly subscriptions** are cancelled effective at the end of the current billing period. You keep access to paid features until the period ends.
  - **Yearly subscriptions** are cancelled effective at the end of the current yearly period. No partial refunds are issued for the remaining months.

  After cancellation, your organisation is moved to the Free plan automatically. Features included in your previous plan remain visible but locked until you re-subscribe.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens to my data after I cancel?">
  Your data is retained for **30 days** after your plan ends or your account is closed. During this window you can still log in, export your kasboek, and download member records.

  After 30 days, your data may be permanently deleted. We strongly recommend **exporting all your data before cancelling**:

  - Export the kasboek as CSV from **Kasboek > Exporteer**
  - Export the member list from **Leden > Exporteer**
  - Download any receipts or documents you need to keep

  If you need a data export in a specific format for handover to a new treasurer, contact support@kaspert.be before cancelling.
</Accordion>
